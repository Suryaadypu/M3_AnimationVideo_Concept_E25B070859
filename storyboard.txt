SCENE 1 — HOOK (0:05–0:48)
VISUAL: A car travels sixty metres in three seconds. Road markers, speedometer, timer, motion streaks, and distance counter move as the car reaches an instant marker.
MATHEMATICS: Average speed = distance ÷ time = 60 ÷ 3 = 20 metres per second. Ask how to measure speed at one exact instant.

SCENE 2 — CONCEPT INTRODUCTION (0:48–1:54)
VISUAL: The parabola draws across an animated coordinate plane. A point sweeps along the curve while its coordinates and tangent indicator update.
MATHEMATICS: Define f(x) = x² and locate A = (1, 1). The goal is the exact slope at x = 1.

SCENE 3 — VISUAL INTUITION (1:54–2:42)
VISUAL: Point B moves toward A. The secant rotates, the run h and rise change, and the curve's tangent direction becomes visible.
MATHEMATICS: Secant slope = [f(1+h) − f(1)] / h. This measures average change between two points.

SCENE 4 — WORKED DERIVATION (2:42–3:27)
VISUAL: Each algebra line writes on screen as the secant moves. Values for h = 1, 0.5, and 0.1 appear beside the graph.
MATHEMATICS: [(1+h)² − 1] / h = [1+2h+h²−1] / h = [2h+h²] / h = 2+h, with h ≠ 0. The sampled slopes are 3, 2.5, and 2.1.

SCENE 5 — INTERPRETATION (3:27–4:20)
VISUAL: The secant settles into the tangent at A. A rise-and-run marker, moving slope indicator, and derivative notation connect the graph to the result.
MATHEMATICS: As h approaches zero, 2+h approaches 2. Therefore f′(1)=2, and for f(x)=x², f′(x)=2x.

SCENE 6 — SUMMARY & CREDITS (4:20–5:07)
VISUAL: Three animated takeaways travel through SECANT → LIMIT → TANGENT. Math reference, original music credit, AI disclosure, and progress line finish on screen.
MATHEMATICS: Secant means average change; the limit brings the points together; the tangent gives the instantaneous slope.
